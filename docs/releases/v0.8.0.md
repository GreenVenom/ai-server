# v0.8.0 Release Notes

## Summary

v0.8.0 completes M08 Backup and Disaster Recovery. The platform now creates encrypted off-host recovery copies for the active V2 Obsidian index and selected non-secret runtime configuration without expanding the server's authority over the user's Obsidian notes.

## Highlights

- Backups are created locally, integrity-recorded, encrypted through the `gdrive_backup_crypt:` remote, verified after upload, and retained according to [ADR-0023](../decisions/ADR-0023-Encrypted-Google-Drive-Off-Host-Backups.md).
- The repository-managed backup job runs daily at 03:30 local time under the `openclaw` account.
- A restore drill verified downloaded checksums, archive scope, a green replacement collection, the `text-dense` 768-dimension Cosine vector contract, 265 matching points, and V2 manifest reconciliation without modifying production.

## Accepted evidence

## Known limitations

- The encrypted backup path depends on the dedicated Google Drive account and separately controlled rclone recovery material.
- The recurring retention audit, simulated low-capacity exercise, quarterly restore drill, and final cleanup of the M08 restore collection remain operational controls rather than one-time release checks.
- The ADR-0022 V1 rollback collection and original manifest remain unchanged through at least 2026-08-26.
